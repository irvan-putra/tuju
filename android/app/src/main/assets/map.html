From the official IDSW 2026 access guideline (idsw.dev/access-guideline), checked Nov 21, 2026.

Car Free Day on Sunday, 22 November: Jl. H.R. Rasuna Said is closed to cars and motorbikes 05:30-09:00 WIB, so cars and ride-hailing cannot drop you at the gate before 09:00, when registration opens. The LRT to Rasuna Said and Transjakarta corridor 6 / 6A to Halte Rasuna Said keep running; if you drive, park at Plaza Festival.

Nearest stops: Stasiun LRT Rasuna Said (LRT Jabodebek) and Halte Transjakarta Rasuna Said (corridor 6 / 6A), each about 2 minutes on foot. This page's 4D route below gets off at the same Rasuna Said stop.

Final walk from either stop: leave by the pedestrian bridge (JPO) toward the Plaza Festival side, walk about 150 m (around 2 min) past Plaza Festival, then enter the Gedung PPHUI complex to reach Usmar Ismail Hall.

Ride-hailing (Gojek / Grab): set the destination to Usmar Ismail Hall, or Gedung Pusat Perfilman H. Usmar Ismail (PPHUI). If the main gate is congested, pin Plaza Festival (South Entrance) next door, about a 1-minute walk.

Parking: the hall has parking for cars and motorbikes but fewer than 100 spaces; the official advice is to park at Plaza Festival next door.

Travel tips: keep at least Rp 20,000 on your e-money card (JakLingko, Mandiri e-Money, BCA Flazz, BNI TapCash or BRI Brizzi). Handy apps: TJ: Transjakarta, Access by KAI, MyMRTJ and Google Maps. Peak hours 07:30-09:30 and 16:30-19:30 WIB: Jl. Prof. Dr. Satrio and Jl. H.R. Rasuna Said are heavily congested - LRT, the busway lanes or a motorbike taxi are usually faster than a car.
