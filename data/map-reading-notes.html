Arrowheads show direction. A line hop or gap at a crossing means the lines do not connect. Hollow rings mark stops. Dashed lines are walking or direction-switch links, not guaranteed paid-area transfers.

Arkadia return A: stay on the venue side

Board 7E at Phe Tower beside the venue toward Ragunan. No bridge crossing is needed for this boarding. The selected 7E leg ends at Department of Agriculture, the same station as Simpang Ragunan Ar-Raudhah. Corridor 6 connects this station and the Ragunan destination pin in both directions.

Home: 6 / 6A toward Galunggung, change at Patra Kuningan to 4D, then get off at Simpang Pramuka 1. Galunggung is the direction, not the transfer. Allow a fresh tap/fare.

Arkadia return B: cross to the far-side pickup

Use the pedestrian bridge to the far-side pickup, then a separate Gojek ride to Flyover Raya Bogor for 7F. Gojek fare/time and 7F boarding access remain unverified. This is separate from the 7E / 6 return option. Check the 7F service and headsign on the day; published hours disagree.

Grand Indonesia

Out: 4C to Tosari 3, then walk about 640 m / 8 min. Home: walk to Tosari 2 for the return 4C. Check the crossing and the correct roadside boarding point.

Central Park and The Breeze BSD

From Central Park, use the JPO for the roughly 450 m / 6 min walk to the S11 boarding point at Jln. Gelong Baru Utara. Home: get off S11 at Taman Anggrek and walk back to Central Park.

S11 ends at Serpong BSD, not at The Breeze. The final walk is about 2.2 km / 29 min via BSD Grand Boulevard. Pedestrian paths and crossings have not been checked on site. Check current service in the TJ app; no live arrivals or last-bus guarantee.

BINUS and Mall Alam Sutera

4C curbside and S61 BRT at Bundaran Senayan require a separate walk via pedestrian access, not an in-platform change. Allow a fresh tap/fare. Check the overpass, gates and correct platform on the day.

  • BINUS main building, Jl. Jalur Sutera Barat Kav. 21: Forest Park to main building about 580 m / 8 min; main building to the return Mall stop about 620 m / 8 min.
  • Mall / BINUS extension, East Side, Kav. 16, first floor: Forest Park to extension about 1 km / 13 min; extension to the return Mall stop about 790 m / 10 min.

These are different buildings within the same BINUS Alam Sutera operation. Walks depend on the entrance. Sidewalks, crossings and gate access remain unverified on site. Short-turn services exist; no promised wait or last bus.
