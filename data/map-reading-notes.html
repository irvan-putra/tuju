Mall Kelapa Gading, home

Mall Kelapa Gading is a TransJakarta stop (B02191P), served by 12P and by the JAK feeders JAK.24, JAK.59, JAK.61 and JAK.115. It is not on a BRT corridor, so every way home needs one change. On the map, 12P, JAK.61 and JAK.59 start at the mall stop at the top and join the corridor 10 and corridor 4 columns at their real stops, so those stops are drawn once; green rings mark where each option gets off (Kayu Putih Rawasari on the 10, Simpang Pramuka 2 on the 4). Stasiun LRT Boulevard Utara is also about 400 m away across the road (not drawn).

Main option: 12P (toward JIS) to Sunter Kelapa Gading Arah Utara, 9 stops, about 21 min scheduled, about every 5 min from 05:00 to 22:00. Walk about 25 m across to Sunter Kelapa Gading Arah Selatan, then corridor 10 toward PGC to Kayu Putih Rawasari, 5 stops, about 26 min scheduled (the stop before Pemuda Pramuka). Kayu Putih Rawasari is the corridor 10 stop closest to home: the map's own walk home from it is about 230 m (Irvan, 26 Nov: it is closer than Pemuda Pramuka). No corridor 4 change is needed.

Alternative 1: JAK.61 (the loop leg toward Pulo Gadung) to Pulo Gadung 5, 14 stops, about 33 min scheduled. Walk about 95 m to Pulo Gadung, then 4 toward Galunggung to Simpang Pramuka 2, 8 stops, about 23 min scheduled. The 4 starts empty at Pulo Gadung.

Alternative 2: JAK.59 (toward Rawamangun) to Simpang Arion, 19 stops, about 46 min scheduled. Walk about 75 m to Pemuda Rawamangun, then 4 toward Galunggung to Simpang Pramuka 2, 4 stops, about 11 min scheduled. Simpang Pramuka 2 is about 100 m from Pramuka BPKP 2.

Confirmed by Irvan on 26 Nov 2026: the 12P to corridor 10 transfer at Sunter Kelapa Gading is inside the paid area, and Irvan reported that it needs no second fare (26 Nov 2026, from riding it). Other fares, gates, other crossings, walk distances and live arrivals are unverified.
