De Entrance, Arkadia Green Park

Jl. TB Simatupang Kav 88, Kebagusan, Pasar Minggu, South Jakarta 12520.

The destination

Schematic, not to scale. All TransJakarta stops listed sit on TB Simatupang right by the complex; firsthand 18 Nov 2026: the venue sits beside the Phe Tower Kebagusan stop, the pedestrian bridge is between the Mujair and Phe Tower stops, and Jln. Gabus Raya (far side, back via the footbridge) and Baung (venue side) are both farther along toward Kampung Rambutan. Busway route - fewer transfers TransJakarta

Schematic, not to scale. Each colored line is one service; the rings mark transfers.

  1. Walk to Halte Simpang Pramuka (corridor 4, ~290 m from the Green Pramuka area).
  2. Corridor 4 toward Galunggung, ride to the end: Halte Galunggung.
  3. Change to corridor 6 toward Ragunan; get off at Simpang Ragunan Ar-Raudhah.
  4. Walk outside to the Masjid Ar-Raudhah curbside stop (~2 min / 115 m per listings; firsthand 18 Nov 2026 a really short walk out of the paid area, paying again); change to 7E toward Kampung Rambutan and get off at Jln. Gabus Raya (a short walk back to the venue via the pedestrian bridge - the stop is on the far side of the road, past the venue toward Kampung Rambutan). On the return toward Ragunan, 7E also stops at Phe Tower Kebagusan (right by the venue) and Baung - both on the same side as the venue, no crossing.
  5. Walk into Arkadia Green Park; De Entrance fronts TB Simatupang.

Checked against TransJakarta's official GTFS feed on Nov 18, 2026: 7E Kampung Rambutan - Ragunan operates 05:00-22:00, about every 25 min - budget the wait. The Kampung Rambutan-bound 7E boards at Masjid Ar-Raudhah curbside (~115 m from Simpang Ragunan Ar-Raudhah halte), not inside the halte - firsthand 18 Nov 2026 reaching it meant exiting the paid area (a really short walk) and paying a fresh fare. The official regular TransJakarta fare is Rp3,500. Corridors 4 and 6 share Halte Galunggung per secondary listings; firsthand the same day the 4-to-6 change needed no additional fare. Boarding points change - check the TJ: Transjakarta app on the day. Walk times per Moovit stop listings and Google Maps walking directions.

If you pass Jln. Gabus Raya - the Flyover Raya Bogor turnaround TransJakarta

Drawn in the all-routes map above: continue down the 7E column past the Arkadia walk, then back up the left column to Baung. 7E is not a loop route: it runs two one-way patterns (Ragunan - Kampung Rambutan), so turning around means getting off and switching to the opposite-direction service. Flyover Raya Bogor is the first stop past the venue area whose name appears in both direction listings, which makes it the shortest turnaround - firsthand 18 Nov 2026 the link between the two directions is inside the paid area, so turning around there needs no exit.

  1. Missed Jln. Gabus Raya? Stay on the Kampung Rambutan-bound 7E and get off at Flyover Raya Bogor (6 stops: Jln. H. Saidi, Jln. Nangka 1, Jln. Muara 1, Plaza Pp Condet 2, RSUD Pasar Rebo 2, Flyover Raya Bogor).
  2. Switch to the Ragunan-bound 7E at Flyover Raya Bogor - the same stop name appears in both direction listings. The listings do not show whether both directions share one halte, stop opposite each other, or stop at different points on Jl. Raya Bogor; firsthand 18 Nov 2026 the link between the two directions is inside the paid area, so the turnaround needs no exit and no new fare.
  3. Ride toward Ragunan and get off at Phe Tower Kebagusan (10 stops: RSUD Pasar Rebo 1, SMAN 104, Plaza Pp Condet 1, Muara Dalam, ACC Simatupang, Telkomsel Jln. Nangka, Jln. Nangka Raya, Gg. Nanas, Baung, Phe Tower Kebagusan) - right by the venue. One stop earlier, Baung, works too.
  4. Walk from Baung ~4 min (285 m) back to De Entrance on the same side of the road - no crossing - per Moovit's De Entrance stop listing; Phe Tower Kebagusan is right by the venue (firsthand 18 Nov 2026).

Checked Nov 18, 2026: Catenary's rendering of the TransJakarta GTFS shows the Flyover Raya Bogor name in both 7E patterns (Kampung Rambutan-bound stop 14 of 21, Ragunan-bound stop 8 of 24), cross-checked against jaGOdetabek's 7E list (stop G00315) - a switch point confirmed firsthand the same day, with the link inside the paid area. The official feed (gtfs.transjakarta.co.id) had no DNS record at edit time, so it could not be re-pulled for this addition; the Kampung Rambutan-bound segment in the data folder remains from today's verified feed snapshot. Per jaGOdetabek's reading of the official service text, only "X dan Y" headsigns are loop routes, so no stay-aboard loop at either 7E terminus is assumed. Headway is about every 25 min in each direction, 05:00-22:00 - budget the wait twice. Riding on to the Kampung Rambutan terminus instead means 13 stops out and 16 back to Baung, versus 6 and 9 here. Walk times: Baung 285 m / ~4 min per Moovit's De Entrance listing (Moovit lists no Phe Tower figure). Firsthand the same day: the venue sits beside the Phe Tower Kebagusan stop; the pedestrian bridge is between the Mujair and Phe Tower stops; Jln. Gabus Raya (far side) and Baung (venue side) are both farther along toward Kampung Rambutan. The same ride the turnaround stayed inside the paid area and needed no new fare.

Going home from Arkadia TransJakarta

Both choices are drawn in the offline all-routes map above, under Going home from Arkadia, with arrows and every intermediate stop in the selected return patterns.

  1. Arkadia-side option: board 7E toward Ragunan at Phe Tower Kebagusan beside the venue. Continue to Ragunan terminus, get off, then board 6 toward Galunggung from its starting end. At Galunggung change to 4 toward Pulo Gadung and get off at Simpang Pramuka 1 for the Green Pramuka area. Starting at a terminus does not guarantee an empty bus or a seat. Exact 7E-to-6 boarding access and paid-area continuity have not been checked, so allow a fresh tap/fare. The 4 return pattern is not the outbound route reversed.
  2. Gojek alternative: use the pedestrian bridge to the far side of Arkadia, take Gojek to Flyover Raya Bogor, then board a 7F serving the Juanda-bound segment. Get off at Kayu Putih Rawasari for the Green Pramuka area. Check that the vehicle serves this stop, as some 7F headsigns describe combined patterns. Gojek fare/time and the exact drop-off-to-platform access are not verified.

7F hours differ by listing and pattern: jaGOdetabek lists combined patterns until 20:30, Moovit lists the Juanda pattern 07:00-08:20, and Traveloka says 05:00-22:00. Confirm the service at your travel hour. Return stop sequences checked 18 Nov 2026 against Catenary's directional GTFS rendering: 7E, 6, 4, 7F. The official GTFS download could not be re-fetched during this correction. No full-trip travel time is verified.

Markas Indonesia

Midpoint Place, Jl. H. Fachrudin, Kp. Bali, Tanah Abang, Central Jakarta 10250.

The destination

Schematic, not to scale. JPO Blok E is the closest TransJakarta stop to the venue (about 300 m per Google Maps walking directions); the feeders there run one way toward the Tanah Abang 2 terminus. Tanah Abang station is shown for orientation only - the route below is busway-only.

Route A - corridor 4 first, one transfer TransJakarta

Schematic, not to scale. Each colored line is one service; the ring marks the transfer.

  1. Walk ~15 min (1.1 km) to Pramuka Sari halte (platform A-B) and tap in.
  2. Corridor 4 (or 4D) toward Galunggung via Saharjo, ~9 min (3 stops) to Matraman.
  3. Tap out of the halte, walk ~1 min to the Matraman 3 curbside stop.
  4. 5M toward Tanah Abang, ~37 min (21 stops) to JPO Blok E.
  5. Walk ~4 min (300 m) to Markas Indonesia in the Midpoint Place building.

Checked on Google Maps around midday Nov 18, 2026: about 1 hr 24 min total, IDR 7,000 per Google - two boardings (corridor 4 inside the halte, then a fresh tap on the curbside 5M feeder); fare figures are Google estimates, not guaranteed. halte.id confirms corridor 4 serves Pramuka Sari and Matraman (Kawasan Matraman). Among the busway-only options Google returned, every checked route ends on the curbside 5M feeder with its own tap; whether a fully paid-area alternative exists was not established. Times change - check the TJ: Transjakarta app on the day.

Route B - shortest walk from home, one transfer TransJakarta

Schematic, not to scale. Each colored line is one service; the ring marks the transfer.

  1. Walk ~12 min (850 m) to Pramuka BPKP 2, the Bundaran Senayan-bound side.
  2. 4C toward Bundaran Senayan, ~16 min (7 stops) to Tugu Proklamasi.
  3. Change at the same curbside halte to 5M toward Tanah Abang, ~32 min (19 stops) to JPO Blok E.
  4. Walk ~4 min (300 m) to Markas Indonesia in the Midpoint Place building.

Checked on Google Maps the morning of Nov 18, 2026: about 1 hr 30 min total, IDR 7,000 per Google - 4C and 5M are both curbside feeder boardings, so this route never enters a paid halte; fare figures are Google estimates, not guaranteed. halte.id confirms 4C boards at Pramuka BPKP 2 and that 4C and 5M share Tugu Proklamasi. Trade-off versus Route A: shorter walk from the Green Pramuka area and a same-stop change, but both legs stay curbside; Route A keeps its first leg inside a paid halte. Times change - check the TJ: Transjakarta app on the day.

Central Park Mall

Jl. Letjen S. Parman Kav 28, Tanjung Duren, Grogol Petamburan, West Jakarta 11470.

The destination

Schematic, not to scale. Halte Tanjung Duren has a separate building per direction (Arah Barat for Lebak Bulus/Pluit-bound arrivals; switch direction means tap out and in again, per halte.id). Gerbang Tol Tj. Duren sits by SOHO Podomoro City, on the mall's west side.

Route A - fewest transfers TransJakarta

Schematic, not to scale. Each colored line is one service; the ring marks the transfer.

  1. Walk ~3 min (230 m) to Kayu Putih Rawasari (platform D-E-F).
  2. Corridor 10 toward PGC, ~35 min (9 stops) to Cawang Sentral.
  3. Change inside the same halte (platform D-E-F to N-P-R, ~1 min) to corridor 9 toward Pluit, ~1 hr 5 min (16 stops) to Tanjung Duren Arah Barat.
  4. Walk ~5 min (350 m) to the mall.

Checked on Google Maps the morning of Nov 18, 2026: about 2 hr total, priced by Google as a single IDR 3,500 trip - the Cawang Sentral change is a platform change inside one paid halte complex (a Kawasan per halte.id), so no new tap. The single-fare reading rests on Google's quote plus the Kawasan listing, not an operator transfer-policy page, and is not guaranteed. Cawang Sentral is an acceptable interchange; the corridor 9 leg is long (16 stops). One busway fare is preferred where possible, but another fare is acceptable when that is not possible. halte.id confirms corridors 8 and 9 both serve Tanjung Duren.

Plaza Festival (next to Usmar Ismail Hall)

Jl. H. R. Rasuna Said, Kuningan, South Jakarta. Next door to Usmar Ismail Hall (Jl. H. R. Rasuna Said Kav. C-22).

The destination

Schematic, not to scale: it does not show which side of the road, or in what order, the buildings sit. The event site idsw.dev says the Rasuna Said stop (formerly GOR Soemantri) and Rasuna Said LRT station are right in front of the hall and that Plaza Festival is next door; Moovit lists the Rasuna Said stop about 100 m (2-3 min) from Musholla Plaza Festival. Exact crossing and entrance not checked on site. The only bus stop actually named Plaza Festival is a one-way 6K feeder stop, not used below.

Route A - no transfer TransJakarta

Schematic, not to scale.

  1. Walk to Simpang Pramuka 2 (about 14 min per Google Maps from Green Pramuka City; the corridor 4 routes above name the same stop at a shorter ~290 m, so check which entrance you use).
  2. Board 4D toward Patra Kuningan (not plain corridor 4, which ends at Galunggung). It rides Pramuka Sari, Utan Kayu, Pasar Genjing, Matraman, Tegalan, Kesatrian, Manggarai Temporer 2, Pasar Rumput, Halimun, Setiabudi Integritas, Kuningan Madya, Karet Kuningan, then get off at Rasuna Said (13 stops, about 54 min in Google Maps' scheduled transit view). Exit via Rasuna Said Akses E.
  3. Walk about 2 min (130 m, Google Maps) to Plaza Festival; the hall is next door.

Two boarding options, not interchangeable: (a) Simpang Pramuka 2, about 14 min walk, 13 stops, about 54 min; (b) Pramuka Sari, about 14 min walk per Google Maps' result, 12 stops, about 50 min. Both exit at Rasuna Said Akses E. They are different stops with different walks, so choose one and use its walk. Return arrives at Simpang Pramuka 1 (about 8 min walk), which is not Pramuka Sari.

No transfer and one fare. 4D runs every day 05:00-22:00, about every 15 min (also shown as "every 15 min" in Google Maps), so budget the wait. The ride time is Google Maps' scheduled transit figure, not live traffic, and a morning departure date was not confirmed for it. Checked Nov 18, 2026 against halte.id's 4D stop list, jaGOdetabek's 4D page and Wikipedia's corridor 4 page, which agree on the stop order; the stop-by-stop list is not from the official GTFS feed, which could not be reached at edit time (no DNS record), so the 4D segment in the data folder and the app map is marked non-GTFS. Simpang Pramuka 2 is the halte.id stop for the Galunggung and Patra Kuningan direction. 4D is a cross-corridor service that is temporarily rerouted via Matraman Raya (Tegalan and Kesatrian) per Wikipedia, so the bus is not the same pattern as plain corridor 4 - check the destination sign.

Fallback - corridor 4, then 6 TransJakarta

  1. Corridor 4 toward Galunggung from Simpang Pramuka 2, ride to the end: Galunggung.
  2. Change to corridor 6 toward Ragunan and get off at Rasuna Said (Setiabudi Integritas, Kuningan Madya, Karet Kuningan, Rasuna Said - 4 stops, from the official GTFS feed checked Nov 18, 2026).

One transfer. Firsthand Nov 18, 2026: the 4-to-6 change at Galunggung needed no additional fare. Use this only if no 4D is coming.

Coming back

  1. From the hall, walk about 3 min (0.2 mi) to the Rasuna Said stop. Google Maps walking directions route this over the pedestrian overpass with stairs: follow that rather than crossing the road at street level.
  2. Board 4D toward Pulo Gadung and ride 10 stops (about 52 min in Google Maps' scheduled transit view) to Simpang Pramuka 1, the Pulo Gadung-direction stop across the road from Simpang Pramuka 2.
  3. Walk about 8 min (0.4 mi, Google Maps) to the Green Pramuka area. Use the signed crossing or overpass at the stop.

No transfer. Rasuna Said is on the Pulo Gadung-bound 4D pattern per Moovit's route PDF, BusMaps and Google Maps; halte.id's list omits it, so check the destination sign. Return stop order and times are not from the official GTFS feed. Never reverse the outbound list.

In a hurry

BlueBird / Gojek straight to the venue: Arkadia Green Park about 30-40 min, Tanah Abang about 35-50 min, Central Park about 40-55 min from the Pramuka area per Google Maps this morning - allow more in rush hour.
