De Entrance, Arkadia Green Park

Jl. TB Simatupang Kav 88, Kebagusan, Pasar Minggu, South Jakarta 12520.

The destination

Schematic, not to scale. All TransJakarta stops listed sit on TB Simatupang right by the complex. Tanjung Barat is the nearest KRL station, shown for orientation only - the route below is busway-only.

Busway route - fewer transfers TransJakarta

Schematic, not to scale. Each colored line is one service; the rings mark transfers.

  1. Walk to Halte Simpang Pramuka (corridor 4, ~290 m from the Green Pramuka area).
  2. Corridor 4 toward Galunggung, ride to the end: Halte Galunggung.
  3. Change to corridor 6 toward Ragunan; get off at Simpang Ragunan Ar-Raudhah.
  4. Walk ~2 min (115 m) outside to the Masjid Ar-Raudhah curbside stop; change to 7E toward Kampung Rambutan and get off at Jln. Gabus Raya (~1 min walk to the venue). On the return toward Ragunan, 7E also stops at Baung and Phe Tower Kebagusan.
  5. Walk into Arkadia Green Park; De Entrance fronts TB Simatupang.

Checked against TransJakarta's official GTFS feed on Nov 18, 2026: 7E Kampung Rambutan - Ragunan operates 05:00-22:00, about every 25 min - budget the wait. The Kampung Rambutan-bound 7E boards at Masjid Ar-Raudhah curbside (~115 m from Simpang Ragunan Ar-Raudhah halte), not inside the halte - that leg boards outside the paid area, and its tap and fare treatment is not verified in operator sources. The official regular TransJakarta fare is Rp3,500. Corridors 4 and 6 share Halte Galunggung per secondary listings; that paid-area continuity remains unconfirmed. Boarding points change - check the TJ: Transjakarta app on the day. Walk times per Moovit stop listings and Google Maps walking directions.

Markas Indonesia

Midpoint Place, Jl. H. Fachrudin, Kp. Bali, Tanah Abang, Central Jakarta 10250.

The destination

Schematic, not to scale. JPO Blok E is the closest TransJakarta stop to the venue (about 300 m per Google Maps walking directions); the feeders there run one way toward the Tanah Abang 2 terminus. Tanah Abang station is shown for orientation only - the route below is busway-only.

Route A - corridor 4 first, one transfer TransJakarta

Schematic, not to scale. Each colored line is one service; the ring marks the transfer.

  1. Walk ~15 min (1.1 km) to Pramuka Sari halte (platform A-B) and tap in.
  2. Corridor 4 (or 4D) toward Galunggung via Saharjo, ~9 min (3 stops) to Matraman.
  3. Tap out of the halte, walk ~1 min to the Matraman 3 curbside stop.
  4. 5M toward Tanah Abang, ~37 min (21 stops) to JPO Blok E.
  5. Walk ~4 min (300 m) to Markas Indonesia in the Midpoint Place building.

Checked on Google Maps around midday Nov 18, 2026: about 1 hr 24 min total, IDR 7,000 per Google - two boardings (corridor 4 inside the halte, then a fresh tap on the curbside 5M feeder); fare figures are Google estimates, not guaranteed. halte.id confirms corridor 4 serves Pramuka Sari and Matraman (Kawasan Matraman). Among the busway-only options Google returned, every checked route ends on the curbside 5M feeder with its own tap; whether a fully paid-area alternative exists was not established. Times change - check the TJ: Transjakarta app on the day.

Route B - shortest walk from home, one transfer TransJakarta

Schematic, not to scale. Each colored line is one service; the ring marks the transfer.

  1. Walk ~12 min (850 m) to Pramuka BPKP 2, the Bundaran Senayan-bound side.
  2. 4C toward Bundaran Senayan, ~16 min (7 stops) to Tugu Proklamasi.
  3. Change at the same curbside halte to 5M toward Tanah Abang, ~32 min (19 stops) to JPO Blok E.
  4. Walk ~4 min (300 m) to Markas Indonesia in the Midpoint Place building.

Checked on Google Maps the morning of Nov 18, 2026: about 1 hr 30 min total, IDR 7,000 per Google - 4C and 5M are both curbside feeder boardings, so this route never enters a paid halte; fare figures are Google estimates, not guaranteed. halte.id confirms 4C boards at Pramuka BPKP 2 and that 4C and 5M share Tugu Proklamasi. Trade-off versus Route A: shorter walk from the Green Pramuka area and a same-stop change, but both legs stay curbside; Route A keeps its first leg inside a paid halte. Times change - check the TJ: Transjakarta app on the day.

Central Park Mall

Jl. Letjen S. Parman Kav 28, Tanjung Duren, Grogol Petamburan, West Jakarta 11470.

The destination

Schematic, not to scale. Halte Tanjung Duren has a separate building per direction (Arah Barat for Lebak Bulus/Pluit-bound arrivals; switch direction means tap out and in again, per halte.id). Gerbang Tol Tj. Duren sits by SOHO Podomoro City, on the mall's west side.

Route A - fewest transfers TransJakarta

Schematic, not to scale. Each colored line is one service; the ring marks the transfer.

  1. Walk ~3 min (230 m) to Kayu Putih Rawasari (platform D-E-F).
  2. Corridor 10 toward PGC, ~35 min (9 stops) to Cawang Sentral.
  3. Change inside the same halte (platform D-E-F to N-P-R, ~1 min) to corridor 9 toward Pluit, ~1 hr 5 min (16 stops) to Tanjung Duren Arah Barat.
  4. Walk ~5 min (350 m) to the mall.

Checked on Google Maps the morning of Nov 18, 2026: about 2 hr total, priced by Google as a single IDR 3,500 trip - the Cawang Sentral change is a platform change inside one paid halte complex (a Kawasan per halte.id), so no new tap. The single-fare reading rests on Google's quote plus the Kawasan listing, not an operator transfer-policy page, and is not guaranteed. Cawang Sentral is an acceptable interchange; the corridor 9 leg is long (16 stops). One busway fare is preferred where possible, but another fare is acceptable when that is not possible. halte.id confirms corridors 8 and 9 both serve Tanjung Duren.

In a hurry

BlueBird / Gojek straight to the venue: Arkadia Green Park about 30-40 min, Tanah Abang about 35-50 min, Central Park about 40-55 min from the Pramuka area per Google Maps this morning - allow more in rush hour.
