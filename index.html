De Entrance, Arkadia Green Park

Jl. TB Simatupang Kav 88, Kebagusan, Pasar Minggu, South Jakarta 12520.

The destination

Schematic, not to scale. All TransJakarta stops listed sit on TB Simatupang right by the complex. Tanjung Barat is the nearest KRL station, shown for orientation only - the route below is busway-only.

Busway route - fewer transfers TransJakarta

Schematic, not to scale. Each colored line is one service; the rings mark transfers.

  1. Walk to Halte Simpang Pramuka (corridor 4, ~290 m from the Green Pramuka area).
  2. Corridor 4 toward Galunggung, ride to the end: Halte Galunggung.
  3. Change to corridor 6 toward Ragunan; get off at Simpang Ragunan Ar-Raudhah.
  4. Change to 7E toward Kampung Rambutan; get off at Jln. Gabus Raya or Phe Tower Kebagusan (both ~1-4 min walk) or Baung.
  5. Walk into Arkadia Green Park; De Entrance fronts TB Simatupang.

Per secondary stop listings checked Nov 18, 2026, corridors 4 and 6 share Halte Galunggung and 7E is listed as boarding inside Simpang Ragunan Ar-Raudhah - so this route may stay inside the paid area on one fare, but that is unconfirmed against current operator info. Boarding points change; check the TJ: Transjakarta app on the day. Corridor 4 and 6 run roughly 05:00-22:00+, 7E about 05:00-22:00 with ~25 min headway - budget the wait. Walk times per Moovit stop listings and Google Maps walking directions.

Markas Indonesia

Midpoint Place, Jl. H. Fachrudin, Kp. Bali, Tanah Abang, Central Jakarta 10250.

The destination

Schematic, not to scale. JPO Blok E is the closest TransJakarta stop to the venue (about 300 m per Google Maps walking directions); the feeders there run one way toward the Tanah Abang 2 terminus. Tanah Abang station is shown for orientation only - the route below is busway-only.

Route A - corridor 4 first, one transfer TransJakarta

Schematic, not to scale. Each colored line is one service; the ring marks the transfer.

  1. Walk ~15 min (1.1 km) to Pramuka Sari halte (platform A-B) and tap in.
  2. Corridor 4 (or 4D) toward Galunggung via Saharjo, ~9 min (3 stops) to Matraman.
  3. Tap out of the halte, walk ~1 min to the Matraman 3 curbside stop.
  4. 5M toward Tanah Abang, ~37 min (21 stops) to JPO Blok E.
  5. Walk ~4 min (300 m) to Markas Indonesia in the Midpoint Place building.

Checked on Google Maps around midday Nov 18, 2026: about 1 hr 24 min total, IDR 7,000 - one Rp3,500 boarding on corridor 4 inside the paid halte, plus a fresh Rp3,500 tap on the curbside 5M feeder (current TransJakarta fare is Rp3,500 per boarding; a fare adjustment starts 1 Jan 2027 with the amount not yet announced - Antara, Oct 2, 2026). halte.id confirms corridor 4 serves Pramuka Sari and Matraman (Kawasan Matraman). The 5M leg is unavoidable: no BRT corridor reaches Tanah Abang's Kp. Bali area, so every busway-only option ends on a curbside feeder with its own fare. Times change - check the TJ: Transjakarta app on the day.

Route B - shortest walk from home, one transfer TransJakarta

Schematic, not to scale. Each colored line is one service; the ring marks the transfer.

  1. Walk ~12 min (850 m) to Pramuka BPKP 2, the Bundaran Senayan-bound side.
  2. 4C toward Bundaran Senayan, ~16 min (7 stops) to Tugu Proklamasi.
  3. Change at the same curbside halte to 5M toward Tanah Abang, ~32 min (19 stops) to JPO Blok E.
  4. Walk ~4 min (300 m) to Markas Indonesia in the Midpoint Place building.

Checked on Google Maps the morning of Nov 18, 2026: about 1 hr 30 min total, IDR 7,000 - 4C and 5M are both curbside feeder boardings at Rp3,500 each, so this route never enters a paid halte (current TransJakarta fare is Rp3,500 per boarding; a fare adjustment starts 1 Jan 2027, amount not yet announced - Antara, Oct 2, 2026). halte.id confirms 4C boards at Pramuka BPKP 2 and that 4C and 5M share Tugu Proklamasi. Trade-off versus Route A: shorter walk from the Green Pramuka area and a same-stop change, but both legs stay curbside; Route A keeps its first leg inside a paid halte. Times change - check the TJ: Transjakarta app on the day.

Central Park Mall

Jl. Letjen S. Parman Kav 28, Tanjung Duren, Grogol Petamburan, West Jakarta 11470.

The destination

Schematic, not to scale. Halte Tanjung Duren has a separate building per direction (Arah Barat for Lebak Bulus/Pluit-bound arrivals; switch direction means tap out and in again, per halte.id). Gerbang Tol Tj. Duren sits by SOHO Podomoro City, on the mall's west side.

Route A - fewest transfers TransJakarta

Schematic, not to scale. Each colored line is one service; the ring marks the transfer.

  1. Walk ~3 min (230 m) to Kayu Putih Rawasari (platform D-E-F).
  2. Corridor 10 toward PGC, ~35 min (9 stops) to Cawang Sentral.
  3. Change inside the same halte (platform D-E-F to N-P-R, ~1 min) to corridor 9 toward Pluit, ~1 hr 5 min (16 stops) to Tanjung Duren Arah Barat.
  4. Walk ~5 min (350 m) to the mall.

Checked on Google Maps the morning of Nov 18, 2026: about 2 hr total, priced by Google as a single IDR 3,500 trip - the Cawang Sentral change is a platform change inside one paid halte complex (a Kawasan per halte.id), so no new tap. That matches the current TransJakarta fare of Rp3,500 per boarding (a fare adjustment starts 1 Jan 2027, amount not yet announced - Antara, Oct 2, 2026); the single-fare reading still rests on Google plus the Kawasan listing, not an operator transfer-policy page. Trade-offs: Cawang Sentral is the Cawang Intermoda interchange and the corridor 9 leg is long (16 stops). halte.id confirms corridors 8 and 9 both serve Tanjung Duren.
