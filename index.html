Getting to De Entrance, Arkadia Green Park - Jl. TB Simatupang Kav 88, Kebagusan, Pasar Minggu, South Jakarta 12520.

The destination

Schematic, not to scale. All TransJakarta stops listed sit on TB Simatupang right by the complex.

Route A - busway only, transfers stay in the paid area TransJakarta fewest exits

  1. Walk to Halte Simpang Pramuka (corridor 4, ~290 m from the Green Pramuka area).
  2. Corridor 4 toward Galunggung, ride to the end: Halte Galunggung.
  3. Transfer inside the paid area to corridor 6 toward Ragunan; get off at Simpang Ragunan Ar-Raudhah.
  4. Transfer inside the halte to 7E toward Kampung Rambutan; get off at Jln. Gabus Raya (~1 min walk) or Baung (~4 min).
  5. Walk into Arkadia Green Park; De Entrance fronts TB Simatupang.

Single TransJakarta fare (Rp3,500, Rp2,000 before 07:00) covers the whole trip while transfers stay inside the paid area / within the transfer time window. Corridor 4 and 6 run roughly 05:00-22:00+, 7E about 05:00-22:00 with ~25 min headway - budget the wait.

Route B - fastest today per Google Maps TransJakarta KRL

  1. Walk ~230 m to Halte Kayu Putih Rawasari (platform D-E-F).
  2. TransJakarta 10 toward PGC, 4 stops (~15 min) to Flyover Jatinegara.
  3. Walk ~9 min to Jatinegara station; KRL Cikarang Line toward Angke, 2 stops to Manggarai.
  4. KRL Bogor Line toward Bogor, 6 stops (~15 min) to Tanjung Barat.
  5. Either walk ~5 min to St. Tj. Barat 2 and take D21 toward Lebak Bulus, 2 stops to Phe Tower Kebagusan (then ~2 min walk), or walk ~20 min straight to Arkadia.

~1h20 total, about IDR 13,000 including the KRL leg. Times are live estimates; check Google Maps or the TJ: Transjakarta app before leaving.

In a hurry

BlueBird / Gojek straight to De Entrance, Arkadia Green Park - roughly a 30-40 min drive from the Pramuka area outside rush hour; the complex fronts TB Simatupang.
